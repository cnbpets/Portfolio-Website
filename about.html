Hi!

My name is Claire Nicole,

My pronouns are she/her and I am a 23 year old canadian artist.

I grew up on the east coast in New Brunswick. After graduating highschool I tried pursuing Science at UNB but found out it really wasn't for me.

I decided to change paths and pursue a career in graphic design at NBCCD, although that didn't exactly go as planned either.

Due to the small class size and large number of applicants I was denied entry into my second year of graphic design and was told I should just go somewhere else.

I always knew from a young age that I wanted to move away to a bigger city and in 2023 I did just that.

I now live in Alberta Canada and have been continuing to pursue a career in design.

I have experience in drawing comissions for people, mostly tattoo designs, but I have yet to sell a piece of my own original work.

If you are interested in viewing my digital drawing process please click on the video below and if you are wanting to see some of my current completed drawings feel free to head over to the gallery page to see more.

Thank you for taking the time to read this and getting to learn more about me.

- Claire Nicole
